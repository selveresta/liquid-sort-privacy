Privacy Policy – Liquid Sort

Effective date: 2026-09-30

This policy explains how the mobile game Liquid Sort (package com.evergreenstudio.liquidsuperpuzzlemegasort), published by Evergreen Studio ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts of its own, no chat and no analytics. Your progress (levels, stars, daily streak and settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads between levels and optional video ads you can choose to watch for bonuses (undo, hint, extra tube), using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device information such as model, OS version and language;
  • interactions with ads (impressions and clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs of the ads SDK.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google Play Games (optional)

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the game reports achievements and leaderboard scores (levels completed, stars) to Google Play Games Services under your Play Games profile. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; you can manage your Play Games profile and its visibility in the Google Play Games app. We do not receive your Google account details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Progress saved on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies; you can manage it at My Ad Center.

Security

All communication between the ads SDK and Google's servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new effective date.
